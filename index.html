AI Scribe

Listens to consultations and drafts structured clinical notes, so doctors spend their time on patients instead of paperwork.

  • Structured summaries ready for review and sign-off
  • Exports to your records system

Tamil AI Industrial Assistant

A voice and chat assistant that speaks Tamil, giving shop-floor teams instant answers on machines, procedures and safety.

  • Ask by voice in Tamil, get answers in Tamil
  • Trained on your SOPs, manuals and checklists
  • Troubleshooting and safety guidance on the spot

03 · QUALITY

DQMS

A digital quality management system that replaces paper checklists and spreadsheets with one traceable, auditable workflow.

  • Inspections, non-conformances and corrective actions
  • Audit trails and document control
  • Live quality dashboards for management
Request a demo →
04 · MOBILITY

Traffic Simulation

Model roads, junctions and signals in 3D, test changes virtually, and see the impact on congestion before anything is built.

  • Junction and corridor scenarios in a digital twin
  • Signal-timing and lane-change experiments
  • Visual reports for planners and stakeholders
Request a demo →
05 · VOICE THERAPY

Puberphonia

A guided voice-therapy app for puberphonia, helping people build a natural, lower-pitched voice with real-time pitch feedback.

  • Live pitch tracking during exercises
  • Structured daily practice programme
  • Progress reports to share with a speech therapist
Request a demo →
06 · DATA SECURITY

Excel Multi-Layer Protection

Layered password protection for Excel files, so each person sees and edits only the parts of a workbook they are allowed to.

  • Separate passwords for file, sheets and ranges
  • Role-based access for teams
  • Keeps sensitive financial and HR data contained
Request a demo →

Tamil Diaspora

A digital home for Tamil people around the world — to connect, share culture, keep the language alive and support each other.

  • Community groups, events and directory
  • Tamil language and culture resources
